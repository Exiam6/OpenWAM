## Training audit and current interpretation

Both pilot variants trained for 1,000 optimizer steps on eight GPUs, with
per-device batch 1 and accumulation 1 (global batch 8). The training log
reports **124,103 training windows**; **14,228 is the dev-window count**.
Earlier conversation estimates incorrectly used the dev count as the train
count. The pilot processed about 8,000 windows, or 0.0645 dataset passes.

At the measured approximately 24.65 seconds per optimizer step, one pass of
this ten-task training dataset would take about 106 GPU-node hours on the
same eight-GPU setup; five passes would take about 531 hours per model.
These are throughput extrapolations, not a verified convergence requirement
or the published RoboTwin training schedule. The five-epoch example in the
upstream fine-tuning guide does not establish the paper's exact recipe.

The VAE control deliberately reset `dit.patch_embedding` and `dit.head.head`.
The RAE initialization copied compatible foundation tensors and replaced
the visual projections for DINOv2 latents. Neither pilot should be described
as an already reproduced, high-performing released baseline. Saved pilot
checkpoints contain weights and deployment assets, but not complete optimizer
state for an exact training continuation.

Offline held-out action flow-matching losses were VAE 0.0183711 versus RAE
0.0235261 on test. These are teacher-forced proxy losses, not rollout action
accuracy or robot success rates. Native rollout inference used 10 denoising
steps and a full 32-action execution horizon. Limited training and error
accumulation are hypotheses; an evaluation mismatch has not been excluded by
a complete official-checkpoint baseline. No RAE improvement is established.
