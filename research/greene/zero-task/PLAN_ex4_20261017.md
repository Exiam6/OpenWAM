Registered 2026-10-17 ~16:10 CDT before any GPU run. Protocol: research_lab/TOP_PRIORITY/EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md.

## Question and research question it serves

Research question (user): can RAE ideas raise RoboTwin closed-loop success? ex1–ex3 could only measure adjust_bottle, because
every arm scored 0 on the other two tasks. This experiment asks: **is the zero caused by the Shenlong execution environment
(independent of the policy), or by the policies?**

So the renderer is ruled out as the cause of the official collapse, and something else in the Shenlong setup differs.

## Hypotheses and competing explanations

- **E (execution environment):** on Shenlong, executing end-effector targets through `take_action(..., 'ee')` (per-step cuRobo
  planning on Blackwell, or another host-specific component) does not reproduce Torch behaviour for these tasks.
- **P (policy):** the execution path is fine; our policies (and the official one on this host, for some other reason) produce
  targets that do not complete the tasks.

## Experiment 4a (this registration): expert end-effector replay through the policy action path

Intervention: none to any model. For each dev demonstration (5 handover_block, 3 place_object_basket, 2 adjust_bottle as
control; seeds 41xx100–104, collected on gpu-02), set up the same scene seed, then send the demonstration's own next recorded
end-effector state (20-dim, the exact policy output format) at every step through `eef20d_to_ee16d` → `take_action(..., 'ee')`,
the same calls `openwam2robotwin_interface.eval` makes. Record planner status per arm, physics ticks, gripper contacts, and the
native success check.

Held fixed: RoboTwin commit, task config `demo_clean`, cuRobo configs, renderer pin, OIDN 2.3.3, step limit. Hosts: gpu-01 GPU 1.

Prediction / criterion:
- If replay succeeds on adjust_bottle but fails on most handover / place demonstrations → **E supported**: the policy action
  path cannot reproduce demonstrations on this host; no policy comparison on these two tasks is meaningful here until fixed.
- If replay succeeds on all three tasks (≥ 4/5 handover, ≥ 2/3 place) → **E refuted for this path**; next is P (open-loop
  generated-action check on dev windows, ex4b, to be registered separately).
- Mixed → report counts and the failing step's planner status; no conclusion beyond the counts.

Budget: 1 GPU, ~30 min, ~5 GB. Stop if the scene's initial state does not match the demonstration's first frame (scene
reproduction failure is reported, not worked around).

## Result of ex4a (2026-10-17 17:40 CDT) and registration of ex4c (before launch)

### ex4a result (expert end-effector replay on gpu-01)

| replay | adjust_bottle | handover_block | place_object_basket | planner failures |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| as recorded | 2/2 | 0/5 | 0/1 | 0 |
| per-step offset compensation (diagnostic) | 1/1 | **4/5** | 0/2 | 0 |

Mechanism found: during the static grasp phase the hand settles ~11 mm (x −11, y +2.3 mm) and ~1° off the recorded pose and
stays there; the controller reaches cuRobo's planned joints exactly (0 rad error) and cuRobo is self-consistent on sm_120
(21/21 plans, 0 mm FK error in its own model), so the offset is a cuRobo-model vs simulator-robot mismatch. The recorded
demonstration pose already contains the offset once; commanding it adds it again; the fingers close off-centre and topple the
block (frames: `records/zero-task-ex4/replay-vs-demo-handover-4101100.jpg`). Compensation restores handover (4/5). Hypothesis
**E supported for handover**; place is not explained by this alone. Open: why the official checkpoint still succeeds on Torch
(bench env torch 2.4.1+cu124 there vs 2.7.0+cu128 here; same SAPIEN) — needs the same replay on Torch (TORCH task).

### ex4c: closed-loop evaluation of existing policies with deploy-time offset compensation

Question: with the same compensation applied at deployment, do our existing policies leave zero on handover_block?
Intervention: at every `take_action`, add to each arm's position target the running sum of (commanded target − reached
position) measured after previous actions in the same episode (reset per episode). Policy, checkpoints, scenes, prompts,
step limits, success rule unchanged. This changes the execution layer, so it is a new evaluation variant, never mixed with ex3.
Held fixed: ex3 frozen fresh scenes, ex3 checkpoints, server settings, episode RNG.
Arms: raw768_native and svae48_native (best ex3 adjust_bottle arms) × seeds 42/43/44; tasks handover_block (primary) and
adjust_bottle (control, to see compensation does not break a working task).
Prediction: handover > 0/60 for at least one arm; adjust_bottle within ±10 pp of ex3.
Criterion: any handover success count ≥ 3/60 per arm is read as "zero was mainly the execution offset"; 0/60 for both arms →
offset is not sufficient and the policy side must be examined (ex4b).
Budget: gpu-01 GPUs 1–4 only (outside 01:00–10:00), two cells per GPU, 6 cells, ~2 h wall. Stop on any infrastructure failure.

### ex4b (registered now, before launch): takeover test

Question: placed at the expert's pre-grasp state, does the policy grasp? Procedure: per dev demonstration (5 handover), replay
the expert end-effector path with compensation up to 5 steps before the expert's first left-gripper close, then hand control
to the policy (compensated execution, native step limit counted from the start) until the limit. Arms raw768_native and
svae48_native, seed 42 checkpoints. Record whether/when the left gripper closes, the right gripper closes, and success.
Reading: policy closes within ~20 steps and sometimes succeeds → the zero comes from approach / compounding error; policy does
not close at a perfect pre-grasp state → it has not learned grasp timing (capacity / representation / data). Budget: 1 GPU
(GPU 4), ~40 min.

### Root cause and close-out of this line (2026-10-17 22:10 CDT)

A parallel session (ex4-e to ex4-h, `/home/zifanz4/openwam-runtime/ex4-*_1017/`) located the exact cause: Shenlong's
`configure_robot_paths` relocates the cuRobo YAMLs to a path without the substring `aloha-agilex`; RoboTwin's
`CuroboPlanner.plan_path` applies the ALOHA base-yaw calibration (left +0.02 rad, right +0.01 rad) only when that substring is
in the path. Restoring the marker (identical YAML bytes): expert replay handover 0/5 → 5/5, place 0/2 → 2/2; official
checkpoint on Shenlong handover 0/5 → 5/5, place 0/5 → 3/5, adjust 2/5 → 5/5; our raw768 and S-VAE: adjust → 15/15 each,
handover and place still 0/15. The ~11 mm offset measured here (ex4a) is this missing calibration; the per-step compensation
of ex4a/ex4c approximated it and agrees in direction (handover replay 4/5, adjust +20 pp, policy handover still 0).
ex4b (takeover) is not launched: GPUs 1–4 are occupied by the parallel session's corrected 900-episode factorial (ex4-g) and
its action diagnostic (ex4-b_actiondiagnostic) covers the policy side. The Torch task is withdrawn. This line stops here.
